# Privacy Policy for Color Clash

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Overview

Color Clash ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in relation to the App.

## Information We Collect

**We collect ZERO personal information.**

Color Clash:
- ❌ Does NOT collect any personal data
- ❌ Does NOT require registration or login
- ❌ Does NOT use analytics or tracking
- ❌ Does NOT share data with third parties
- ❌ Does NOT use advertising networks
- ❌ Does NOT access device sensors (except haptics)
- ❌ Does NOT require internet connection

## Local Storage

The App stores **only** your high score locally on your device using browser LocalStorage. This data:
- ✅ Never leaves your device
- ✅ Is not transmitted anywhere
- ✅ Can be deleted by clearing browser data
- ✅ Is not accessible to us or anyone else

## Permissions

The App may request:
- **Haptic Feedback**: Optional vibration for game feedback
  - Only activates when you tap
  - Can be disabled in device settings
  - No data collected

## Third-Party Services

Color Clash does NOT use any third-party services, including:
- Analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Social media integrations
- Cloud services
- Payment processors (app is free)

## Children's Privacy

Color Clash is safe for all ages (rated 4+). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data Security

Since we don't collect any data, there's no risk of your personal information being:
- Lost
- Stolen
- Misused
- Shared
- Sold

Your high score is stored only on your device and never transmitted.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will:
1. Update this policy
2. Update the "Last Updated" date
3. Notify users via app update notes

We will never introduce data collection without explicit user consent.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- ✅ Delete your high score (clear browser data)
- ✅ Use the app without any data collection
- ✅ Play completely offline
