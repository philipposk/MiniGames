      HOW TO PLAY

      Two colored bars slide from opposite sides.

      Tap when they overlap in the center!

      SCORING

      • PERFECT (full overlap): +100
      • GREAT (most overlap): +70
      • GOOD (some overlap): +40
      • OKAY (barely): +15
      • MISS: Game Over (except in ZEN)

      Build combos for bigger multipliers. Earn coins to unlock palettes & shapes.
