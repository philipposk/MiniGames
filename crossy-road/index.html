    How to Play

    • Tap the top of the screen to hop forward.
    • Swipe in any direction to move.
    • Keyboard: arrow keys, WASD, space to hop, P to pause.
    • Dodge cars, ride logs, watch for trains.
    • Stay on screen. Don't fall behind for more than 2 seconds.
    • Collect coins to unlock new characters.
