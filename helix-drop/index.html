HOW TO PLAY

Drag the screen left or right to spin the tower.

Find the gap between segments and let the ball drop through.

Land on green = safe bounce. Hit red = game over.

Chain drops without bouncing for a combo multiplier.

Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ to rotate, P or Esc to pause.
