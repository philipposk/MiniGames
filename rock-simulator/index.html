HOW TO PLAY

1. Tap the rock

Every tap knocks loose a bit of grit. Grit is the whole economy. Yes, that is the entire skill ceiling.

2. Buy erosion

Rain, frost, rivers, glaciers, tectonics. Each one grinds grit off you while you do nothing at all.

3. Grow moss

Moss only grows while you are not tapping. Put the phone down for a minute and come back richer. Moss buys permanent multipliers.

4. Catch cracks

Cracks open up on your surface at random. Tap one before it seals for a burst of grit or a frenzy.

5. Evolve

Enough lifetime grit and you graduate: Pebble → Stone → Boulder → Monolith → Meteorite → Mountain → Planetoid → Continent. Each era multiplies everything.

6. Erode

Prestige. Crumble back to a pebble, keep sediment, and every future run is permanently faster.

Offline

You keep eroding while the tab is closed — up to 8 hours of grit and 12 hours of moss. Rocks are patient.

CREDITS

Rock Simulator — part of the MiniGames arcade.

Vanilla JS. No engine, no ads, no trackers, no network calls unless you opt into the global leaderboard. Works fully offline.

Sound is synthesised live with WebAudio. Every rock is procedurally generated from your save seed, so yours is genuinely yours.
