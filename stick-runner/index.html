HOW TO PLAY

  • JUMP over ground spikes. Tap, Space, or ↑.
  • SLIDE under ceiling spikes. Swipe down or ↓.
  • DOUBLE-JUMP for a front flip. Tap a second time in the air.
  • BACKFLIP with ← (left arrow).
  • BOOST with → (right arrow).
  • PAUSE with P. MENU with Esc.
  • Earn coins every 10m and from close calls. Spend them in the shop.
  • Reach distance milestones to unlock biomes and characters.
