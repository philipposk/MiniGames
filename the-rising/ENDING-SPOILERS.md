## The Journey

### Levels 1-5: The Climb
- Stack humans on boats/rafts/mattresses
- Rising water creates urgency
- Each level freezes, trapping the dead below
- More survivors needed each level
- Visible layers of frozen victims pile up

---

## THE ENDING SEQUENCE

After completing **Level 5**, an epic multi-phase ending unfolds:

### Phase 1: **ASCENT TO HEAVEN** ☁️
- Screen fades to bright sky
- Clouds descend (heaven coming down)
- Peaceful, ethereal atmosphere
- Text: "ASCENDING..."

### Phase 2: **THE PEARLY GATES** ✨
- Beautiful golden gates appear
- Fluffy white clouds everywhere
- Heavenly golden sky with soft blues
- St. Peter stands on a cloud platform

### Phase 3: **ST. PETER APPEARS** 👴
**Comic-style character design:**
- White flowing robes
- Golden halo
- Long white beard and mustache
- Initially calm and welcoming

### Phase 4: **THE REALIZATION** 😨
St. Peter starts to process what happened...

Speech bubble appears with:
1. `"..."` (processing)
2. `"BUT YOU LET"` (realization dawning)
3. `"THEM ALL DIEEEEE!!!"` 💀😱😭

**Visual effects:**
- Screen shakes violently
- St. Peter's eyes go wide
- Arms raised in shock/horror
- Mouth open shouting

### Phase 5: **THE SPARTA KICK** 🦵
**"THIS IS HEAVEN!!!"**

- St. Peter's leg appears (giant boot)
- Player character gets SPARTA-KICKED
- Character spins through the air falling
- Heaven recedes into the distance

### Phase 6: **THE FALL** 🔥
**Descending back to Earth:**
- Screen goes dark
- Flames rise from below
- Text: "THE ICE HAS MELTED"
- All the frozen layers are gone - just fire
- Falling... falling... falling...

### Phase 7: **THE TWIST** 🛏️
**Sudden cut to:**
- Dark bedroom
- Person in bed
- Eyes wide open
- *GASP!*
- Sweat drops on forehead

**Text reveals:**
> "It was all a dream..."

### Phase 8: **THE COMFORT** 💙
Final banner descends with message:

```
IT WILL ALL BE OKAY
shhh...
```

💤 😌 💙

## The Meaning

### Dark Humor + Emotional Weight:
1. **The guilt is real** - You DO let people die throughout the game
2. **No escape from consequences** - Even heaven judges you
3. **The absurdist twist** - Kicked out of heaven Sparta-style
4. **The relief** - It was just a nightmare
5. **The comfort** - Everything will be okay

### Why it Works:
- Respects the emotional journey
- Acknowledges the dark theme
- Provides comic relief (St. Peter's reaction)
- Offers redemption (dream reveal)
- Ends with comfort, not trauma

## Technical Details

### Ending Phases:
1. `ascent` - 3 seconds
2. `gates` - 2 seconds (St. Peter appears)
3. `peter-angry` - 4 seconds (speech + reaction)
4. `kicked` - Dynamic (until player falls off-screen)
5. `falling` - 3 seconds (flames below)
6. `wakeup` - 4 seconds (bedroom scene)
7. `banner` - Infinite (final message)

### Trigger:
- Completing Level 5 → Shows "ASCENDING TO HEAVEN..."
- 2 second delay
- Ending sequence begins
- Player cannot control anything during ending
- Plays automatically like a cutscene
