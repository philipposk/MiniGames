How to Play

The water rises. Stack floating blocks to climb toward the GOAL line. Tap or press SPACE to drop a moving block. Match overlap closely to save more people. Reach the GOAL line before the water reaches your hero.

Controls

TAP / SPACE / ENTER — Place block
P or ESC — Pause
R — Restart
M — Mute
+ / - — Volume
Arrows — Navigate world map
